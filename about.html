About Us

We are a team of four dedicated women in the computer science and engineering field hailing from Kolkata, embarking on this exciting new project.

We believe strongly in the importance of breast cancer awareness and have made it our goal to simplify access to vital resources. With our platform, users can easily receive breast cancer predictions and arrange for MRI scanning, all in one convenient place.

As we continuously learn and evolve, our mission is to provide ever-better services to our users. Please note that, we prioritize your health and safety and your data is safe with us.

We hope that we are able to help as many people as we can, hence we kindly request you to take the test yourself and share it with your loved ones too!

After all, prevention is always better than cure!
