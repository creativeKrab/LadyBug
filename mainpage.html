Your Health is our Priority!

Our platform is designed to provide an accessible and user-friendly way to assess your risk of breast cancer based on your symptoms and additional imaging analysis.

All you need to do is to answer a series of simple Yes or No questions about your symptoms.

These questions are tailored to identify common indicators of breast cancer.

Your responses will help us provide an initial assessment.

If you have access to MRI or other breast imaging scans, you can upload them securely on our platform. Our advanced AI algorithms will analyze these images to predict whether any abnormalities are benign (non-cancerous) or malignant (cancerous).

Once you’ve completed the questionnaire and/or uploaded your MRI images, our system will:

  1. Provide a summary of your symptoms and their potential correlation to breast cancer risk.
  2. Deliver a detailed report from the image analysis, indicating whether any findings suggest benign or malignant outcomes.

Start your assessment today and take the first step toward better breast health!

Important Note

Our platform is designed to offer informational support and early insights. It is not a substitute for professional medical advice or diagnosis. If your results raise concerns, we strongly recommend consulting a licensed healthcare provider for further evaluation and testing.

Your Privacy is Our Policy

We take your privacy seriously. All your data, including answers to the symptom questionnaire and uploaded images, is handled with the utmost confidentiality and in compliance with health data regulations.
